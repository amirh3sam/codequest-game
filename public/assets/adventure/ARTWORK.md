### Warrior atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production animation sprite sheet for a top-down 2D fantasy adventure game. Create ONE transparent PNG, square, exactly 4 columns by 4 rows of equally sized cells (16 full-body sprites). Each cell has ample transparent padding and exactly one armored warrior, same character, same scale, same pixel grid, feet at the same baseline within each cell. NO text, NO grid lines, NO shadows, NO ground, NO scenery. Transparent background is essential. Character: attractive adult-proportioned fantasy knight, visible LONG legs and tall boots, head only about one fifth of the full height, broad but proportionate shoulders, fitted silver-blue steel armor with gold edging, steel helmet with a narrow visor and a crimson plume, a short flowing crimson cape, a straight silver sword with a gold crossguard visibly HELD IN THE RIGHT HAND, small dark teal round shield on left arm. Entire sword, helmet plume, feet all inside the cell. Style: premium richly shaded hand-painted pixel art of a polished 32-bit adventure RPG; elegant shape language, readable silhouette, detailed plate joints, boots, metal highlights, warm rich colors, consistent directional daylight. NOT chibi, NOT cute tiny legs, NOT large head, NOT voxel, NOT isometric, NOT realistic photograph. Camera is standard overhead 3/4 top-down orthographic, body seen enough from the front to clearly show waist, thighs, knees and boots. Row 1 all four sprites face SOUTH (toward viewer). Row 2 all four sprites face WEST (left). Row 3 all four sprites face EAST (right). Row 4 all four sprites face NORTH (away, back of cape visible). Columns in every row: column 1 neutral standing idle, column 2 walking left leg forward and right leg back, column 3 neutral passing stride, column 4 walking right leg forward and left leg back. Walking poses must clearly move BOTH legs and sword arm coherently, without changing character proportions or size. Do not draw a decorative border. Give the warrior slender longer legs, approximately half the body height below belt. Perfectly regular equal-cell sprite atlas.
